# Parent & Student Portal Guide

*(Written to be read aloud at PTA meetings.)*

## What you can see, free, on any smartphone

- Whether your child was in school today, and every day this term
- Fees: what is billed, what you have paid (every receipt), what is left
- Report cards the moment the school publishes them — no more waiting
- School announcements, events and the exam timetable
- Any discipline notes the school shares with you

You also receive **SMS automatically** — even without a smartphone — when your
child is absent, when fees are near due, when a payment is received, and when
report cards are ready.

## How to sign in

1. Give the school office your **phone number** when you register your child —
   that number *is* your account.
2. Open the school's web address in the phone's browser (ask the office for the
   exact address, e.g. `school.example.lr`).
3. Tap **Parent / Student**, enter your phone number and your **PIN**.
   - Your first PIN is the **last 4 digits of your phone number** unless the
     office gave you a different one.
   - Change it right away: ⚙️ → *Change my PIN*.
4. Android: the browser will offer **Add to Home screen** — accept, and the
   portal becomes an app icon that opens instantly and works even with weak
   network.

## Reading the home screen

Each child shows four boxes:

| Box | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Days present / absent** | this term's attendance. Tap *Details* for the day-by-day record |
| **Fees balance** | what is still owed. Tap *Details* to see every payment and receipt number |
| **Average %** | the latest published report card, with class rank |

Tap **📄 Report card** to see the full card and print it or save it as PDF.

## If something looks wrong

- Absent but you brought them to school? Call the school the same day — the
  number is at the top of the portal.
- A payment is missing? Bring the receipt (paper or the SMS) to the bursar —
  every receipt has a number they can check instantly.
- Forgot your PIN? The school office can reset it — they will ask you to
  confirm who you are.

## Older students (secondary)

Schools may give students their own login (their phone + PIN). Students see
their own attendance, results and the library, but never fees of other
students or any other child's records.
